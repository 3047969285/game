Developing a cause-and-effect essay
Every day we ask questions and look for answers. We realize that an event does not occur without
Causes, and that it can have a series of effects. By examining the causes or effects of an event, we seek
to understand and explain things that happen in our lives. The purpose of cause-and-effect essays is
to explain to the reader: 1） the causes of an event; 2） the effects of an event; or 3） both causes and
effects. In B2U6, you have gained some basic knowledge of cause-and-effect essays. In this unit, the
focus will be on the causes of an event.
The usual logical pattern of a causal analysis essay is like this：
Cause 2
Cause 3
Cause 1
Effect
Text A of this unit is a typical causal analysis essay. Para. 1 is an interesting opening, attracting the
reacers attention. In Para. 2, the author puts forward the thesis question: Why do we travel? Then
Trom Para. 3 to Para. 8, the author provides an analysis of the reasons why we travel: First, we need
to travel; second, we want to travel; and thira, we can become more creative. However, the three
causes are not of equal importance. The first two are direct causes and we can think of them easily
While the last reason seems a Iittle beyond our reach. The author apparently takes the third Cause as
something more important, and revealing this cause to the reader is the main purpose of Text A. The
main body of the text is dedicated to the explanation of it. The last paragraph reaches a concilusion，
