Unit project
Conducting a survey on happiness at work
Happiness at work is important. It affects people's work performance, job
satisfaction, and even happiness outside of work. To deepen your understanding of
it, in this unit project, you are expected to prepare a questionnaire and conduct a
survey regarding the happiness level of a certain occupation.
Step 1
Work in groups of four or five and discuss the factors that contribute to
happiness at work. You may turn to the Internet for help.
Step 2
Design a questionnaire that will measure happiness at work. The
