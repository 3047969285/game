Developing an argumentative essay
In writing an argumentative essay, the writer attempts to make a strong argument for or against
a controversial point or defend a position on which there is a difference of opinion. Generally，
the writer starts by stating a position on an issue, and then gives reasons, supported by evidence，
for agreeing with that position. In this unit, you Will learn about the typical structure of an
argumentative essay and some specific tips on now to write it.
A good argumentative essay may follow the following structure：
1 Introduction: The first part of your essay should introduce the topic with a brief explanation
of it, some background information, and a thesis statement. Your thesis is a statement of your
position on a specific controversial topic, and it snould be a concise, one-sentence summary of
your main point or Ciaim.
2 Boay: This part usually comprises several paragraphs that explain the reasons why you
support your thesis. Each paragraph should explain a different idea or give a different piece of
evidence. You could use the patterns of development such as exemplification, comparison /
contrast, ana cause / effect that have been talked about previously.
3 Refutation /Opposition （optional）： You can aiso aaaress opposing points of view ana
explain why you disagree with them. Considering a topic from different angles adds credibility
