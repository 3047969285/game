In this unit, the technique for writing cause-ana-effect essays is explored in terms of organization.
When writing a cause-ana-effect essay, you have to consider two strategic points： （1） whether
you are exploring causes or effects, or both, and （2） what oroer of the causes or etfects you are
going to follow, e.g. from the least to the most important or vice versa. Unlike the texts in B2U6

ettects, namely now the economiC crisis greatly attects almost every aspect of the lives of
