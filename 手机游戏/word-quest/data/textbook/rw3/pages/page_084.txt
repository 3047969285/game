Developing a cause-and-effect essay
Every day we ask questions and look for answers. We realize that an event does not occur without
Causes, and that it can have a series of effects. By examining the causes or effects of an event, we seek
to understand and explain things that happen in our lives. The purpose of cause-and-effect essays is
to explain to the reader: 1） the causes of an event; 2） the effects of an event; or 3） both causes and
effects. In B2U6, you have gained some basic knowledge of cause-and-effect essays. In this unit, the
focus will be on the causes of an event.
