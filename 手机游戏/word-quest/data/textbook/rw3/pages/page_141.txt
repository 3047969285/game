4 Nobody had ever seen the moon's far side before the Soviet Union launched the Luna 3
probe in 1959. It was the first-ever mission to photograph the far side. The United
States Apollo 8 mission sent three astronauts around the moon in 1968, the first time
humans saw the moon's far side with their own eyes. Now China sent the first-ever
probe to soft-land and rove on the far side, which has deepened our understanding of
the moon, the earth, and the universe.
5 Between the 1950s and 1970s, the United States and the Soviet Union raced against
each other in early Iunar exploration. After a lull, new interest was triggered in the
1990s when scientists found there might be water on the moon. Since the turn of the
millennium, more countries such as China, Japan, and India have joined the lunar
exploration club. To date, the world has launched over 100 manned or unmanned
missions to the moon.
6 Flying to the moon has been a dream of the Chinese since ancient times. The moon
has long been a major theme in Chinese poems, music, and paintings, with artists and
writers using the moon to express their emotions and thoughts about the universe.
Since the 1990s, Chinese scientists and engineers have endeavored to make the dream a
reality. China announced in 2000 that its space planning agenda contained preparatory
research about lunar exploration. It officially began the lunar exploration program in
2004, with the aim of orbiting and landing on the moon, and bringing samples back to
the earth. The program was named after the moon goddess Chang'e, who, in Chinese
mythology, lived on the moon and was accompanied by the Jade Rabbit.
7 Although China is a latecomer in lunar exploration, its program has made many
achievements in a few expeditions. The country verified its lunar probe technologies
in 2007 with the successful launch of Chang'e-l, the first spacecraft stemming from
the program. In 2013 Chang'e-3 accomplished China's first soft-landing on the moon，
which was the first man-made craft to achieve this goal in nearly four decades.
