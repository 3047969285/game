Besides wnat nas been illustrated above, here are some tips for writing a comparison / contrast essay.
• The thesis Should present a significant point. You don'tjust list the similarities or differences. Your
thesis statement should explain the significance of your comparison or contrast.
• The two subjects being compared or contrasted should be equally treated. No matter whether
you are using the point-py-point pattern or subject-by-subject pattern, you should explain each
subject fully by providing sufficient exampres and detais.
• Transitional words or phrases should be used wisely when you move from one point to another or
from one subject to the other.
