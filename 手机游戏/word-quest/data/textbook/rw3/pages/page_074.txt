the discomforts of travel. Every journey
is filled with new experiences. Some
are pleasant, even amazing; others are
challenging, even dangerous, especially
When we are traveling alone. So, why not
Just stay sately at home, reading travel

Mark Twain, the famous writer, once
said， “Travel is fatal to prejudice ...and
narrow-mindedness ...” And scientists
studying the brain have come to agree
with his statement. They believe that
travel to foreign places not only provides
gereat memorles out amazingly, causes
changes in our brains and our perceptions.
Consequently, people are more curious
and open to the new, which is the key to
creativity and innovation.
