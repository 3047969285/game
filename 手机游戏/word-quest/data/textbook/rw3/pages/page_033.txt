Following the structure of a text
The structure of a text, which heps to fulfill the author's purpose in writing, refers to the internal

organized influences the comprehension of the text. Readers who are familiar with the structure
of a text expect the information to unfold in certain ways, and they can better recognize the
author's purpose and follow the author's ideas efficiently.
