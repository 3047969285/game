Developing a biographical narrative essay
A biographical narrative essay describes the life of a person. It narrates, analyzes, and interprets
the most important facts of someone's life, ranging from the person's chilchood, eaucational
background, adolescence, professional career, to the person's most outstanding achievements.
Generally, most biographical narrative essays are written in chronological order. Different life
periods in time sequence, usually grouped around a major theme （Such as overcoming adversity.
noteworthy achievements, or adventurous experiences）， are narrated with the purpose of inspiring
or encouraging the reader.

The following steps can help you develop a biographical narrative essay in chronological order.
1 Choose a person you would like to write about.
2 Search for important details about the person.
3 Make an outline, make up a list of all the details, and then arrange these details according to
the order in which they occurrea. Select only those that can best portray the person you are
describing and ignore the unimportant ones.
4 Write a rough draft based on the detailts you have chosen. Use time order signals like when，
during, and later to connect details as you move from the beginning to the end of your narrative.
5 Revise and proofread the draft until you think the narrative is coherent and well developed.
