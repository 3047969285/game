Further developing a cause-and-effect essay
In this unit, the technique for writing cause-ana-effect essays is explored in terms of organization.
When writing a cause-ana-effect essay, you have to consider two strategic points： （1） whether
you are exploring causes or effects, or both, and （2） what oroer of the causes or etfects you are
going to follow, e.g. from the least to the most important or vice versa. Unlike the texts in B2U6
and B3U3, in whicn the authors intend to explore causes, Text A in this unit mainly presents
ettects, namely now the economiC crisis greatly attects almost every aspect of the lives of
indiviauals, through the story of Sue Johnson.
Para. 1 is the introduction of the text, introducing the root cause of the economic slump and the
great impact of the crisis - so many people experienced long months of financial qifficulties. And
the rest of the story touches upon how the crisis affected inaiviauars, such as Sue. The eftects are
explored in detail from different aspects of Sue's life in Paras. 7-11. The following analysis （Paras.
7-11） explains what happened to Sue as a result of the economic crisis.
Effects
• Her loss of income （Para. 7）
• Her loss of a high standard of living （Para. 8）
• Her loss of an opportunity for an education （Para.9）
• Her loss of shelter （Para. 10）
• Her loss of belief and confidence in life （Para. 11）
The effects, as can be seen, are arranged from those on everyday life such as the loss of income
and a high standara of living to the psychological consequence: the loss of belief and confidence
