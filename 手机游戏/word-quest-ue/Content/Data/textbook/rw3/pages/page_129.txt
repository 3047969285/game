Culture notes
Tangong space station: China's first long-term space station in low earth orbit
between 340 and 450 km above the surtace. It aims at creating a national space
laboratory and platform for international scientific as well as technological cooperation.
August 1 Medal: a medal granted to military personnel who have made outstanding
contributions to safeguarding national sovereignty, security, and development interests，
and to advancing the modernization of national defense and the armed forces.
