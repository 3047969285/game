Writing a proffle articie
A profile article is a feature article about a person. It focuses on
a specific trait, characteristic, or noteworthy accomplishment
of that individual. In this unit project, you are going to write
Mencius
a profile article of a famous Chinese person who has made
special contributions to society.
Read the following information to better understand a profile article.
Step 1
A profile article explores the background and character of a
particular person. Its focus should be on a single aspect of the
personal or professional life of the person and this should be
the center that pulls the article together. You can include the
background information, but focus on something interesting.
Generally, a profile article has a lead, body, and closure. The
Iead is the introduction to your article and your subject, i.e.， the
person you are going to write about. It can be an anecdote,a
description, a quote, a question, a comparison, or anything that
draws your reader to the article. The body of a profile article，
which weaves background material with details and quotes，
could be organized thematically or chronologically. To wrap
up a profile article, there are a number of easy ways. One is to
have a circular ending by referring back to your lead. Another
way is to describe a scene or give a summary statement.
Certainly, it is also a good idea to end your article with an
interesting quote from your subject which will leave a deep
Impression on your reader.
Remember, it is of vital importance to select eye-catching detais
about the person and organize them in a creative and interesting
way. To achieve this, what takes place before the writing process
iS crucial Since careful preparation is the basis for any good article.
