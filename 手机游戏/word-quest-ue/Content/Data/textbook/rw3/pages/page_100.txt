AS you grow up and become adults you learn
to work. It may be a simple job helping
your parents, your teachers, the neighbor
next door, or a joo you take up as a career.
Whatever job you do, just as “building
a high-rise begins with mounds of soil"，
you have to take one step at a time and
approach your work with a firm footing.
Nowadays, most people work hard, but
some may enjoy their work while others
may find the work diffrcult and tiring.
There are also differences in the way
people deal with their work. While some
Just work for the sake of money, others，
always keeping people's needs in mind，
may try to build their self-esteem，
responsibility, and pride into their
work. What do you look for in ajob？
What attitudes toward work do you think
are appropriate? How are you going to
deaicate yourself to your work and have
pride in it？
